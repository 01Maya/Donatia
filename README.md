# 💗Donatia — Charity & Nonprofit Landing Page

> A modern, welcoming nonprofit landing page designed to communicate social impact, highlight community initiatives, and encourage people to make a difference.

## ✨ Overview

Donatia is a charity landing page concept focused on **community, compassion, and meaningful change**.  
It combines a clean visual layout, warm imagery, and clear calls to action to present the organization's mission.

---

## 📝 Description

The website introduces Donatia through a friendly, image-led experience with a soft pastel palette and rounded UI elements.  
Visitors can explore the organization's mission, impact statistics, guiding principles, initiatives, testimonials, and recent projects through a structured page flow.  
Smooth scrolling, entrance animations, hover interactions, animated counters, and a curtain-style scroll effect add motion while keeping the experience approachable and easy to follow.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📱 Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- 🧭 Section-based navigation
- ✨ Scroll-reveal and entrance animations
- 🎞️ Curtain-style page-scroll animation
- 🔢 Animated impact counters
- 🖱️ Interactive hover states and micro-interactions
- 🌱 Mission and organization overview
- 📊 Impact statistics to highlight progress
- 🤝 Principles and approach section
- 🐾 Image-led initiative cards
- 💬 Testimonials to support trust and credibility
- 🗂️ Recent projects and impact stories
- 🎯 Clear donation and engagement calls to action
- 📐 Consistent spacing, typography, and visual hierarchy
- 🎨 Soft pastel colors and rounded components
- ♿ Thoughtful, accessible interaction patterns

---

## 🧩 Sections

### 🏠 Hero

Introduces Donatia with the headline “Together, we build a brighter future,” a prominent visual, and a clear call to action.  
Creates an immediate emotional connection and communicates the purpose of the page.

### 📊 Impact Statistics

Highlights key impact figures, including people reached, initiatives, and broader community impact.  
Animated counters draw attention to the numbers as visitors explore the page.

### 💜 About Donatia

Introduces the organization and its mission through concise copy and an image-led layout.  
Helps visitors understand the cause and the need for collective action.

### 💡 How We Deliver Change

Presents the core principles guiding Donatia's work.  
Makes the organization's approach easy to scan and understand.

### 🤝 Impact Initiatives

Showcases initiatives such as animal rescue, environmental action, and school support.  
Uses visual cards to help visitors explore different areas of impact.

### 💬 Testimonials

Shares supporter or community perspectives in a dedicated section.  
Adds a human element to the story and reinforces the importance of the work.

### 🗂️ Latest Projects

Features recent projects, including wildlife conservation, green village efforts, and school meal programs.  
Gives visitors concrete examples of how community-focused initiatives can create change.

### 🎯 Call to Action

Encourages visitors to contribute and support the mission with the message “Your gift changes lives.”  
Provides a clear next step for people who want to get involved.

### 💜 Footer

Provides useful navigation links and a community signup area.  
Closes the page with a structured layout and a way to stay connected.
